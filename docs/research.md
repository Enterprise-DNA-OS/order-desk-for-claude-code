# Research, 19 October 2026

Goflow belongs to merchant order management. Existing Mintsoft and CartonCloud builds serve third-party warehouse operations. This build serves the seller's channel order, delivery promise and return desk. It does not reproduce a warehouse management system.

Selection scores: bill 3, data-shaped 5, buyer evidence 2, search 3, one-session buildable 4, import 5. Total 22/30. Public pricing establishes a five-figure plan. No customer invoice was verified. Local business delivery records show order exception needs, but no verified Goflow customer was found. No live CRM read was made. Search result pages establish alternative-search intent, not measured monthly volume. Buyer and search scores remain modest.

- [Current vendor pricing](https://goflow.com/pricing): the page's calculator configuration includes Thriving, price 2249 USD per month. Its public USD billing note establishes currency. The calculator supports 25,000 retail and 200 wholesale orders on that plan. Twelve months is 26,988 USD. Fees and overages are additional. See pricing-evidence.json for the captured plan object. The old Scale label in directory listings is not used.
- [Custom exports](https://goflow.com/docs/general/custom-exports): configurable line-level CSV supports a reviewed one-command import.
- [Order and returns functions](https://goflow.com/features/sales-orders-returns): vendor context for the order desk.
- [Shipment tracking](https://goflow.com/docs/shipments/tracking): dispatch and delivery are separate events.
- [Amazon Australia connection](https://goflow.com/integrations/amazon-au): ANZ relevance, not evidence that one of our buyers pays for Goflow.
- [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/goflow/reviews): reviews include learning-curve and integration concerns. They are individual opinions, not facts asserted on our comparison page.
- [Alternative search results](https://www.capterra.com/p/182932/goflow/alternatives/): intent exists. No keyword volume claim.
